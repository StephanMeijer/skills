# Message patterns

Skeletons, not templates. Rewrite every line in the user's voice; keep the order.

## First contact with a commercial party

Purpose: turn a public-interest problem into a lead the recipient's job rewards.

```
Subject: <the opportunity, stated as the recipient would state it>

<Hook: something the company said or published this week, or its known footprint nearby.>
<Who writes, in what capacity, and what they are not.>

<The gap in one paragraph, with the one number: population, distance, density, sales, users.>
<Why the gap exists, citing the institution's own study or rule, so the reader can verify.>

Three questions:
1. Is <place/segment> of interest to <company> in principle?
2. If so, would you say so to <the public body>? A signal from <a company> weighs more than one from <residents>. I can introduce you to the right people.
3. What does <company> require of a site like this (<size, logistics, visibility, parking>)? I will pass it on to <owners/developers>.

If a colleague is the right person, I would be glad to hear who.

Sources:
- <study>, <section, page>: <url>
- <official page>: <url>

<Name, address, phone, e-mail>
```

## First contact with an ally institution

Purpose: shared problem, offer collaboration, ask for their experience.

```
<The problem as they experience it daily.>
<Short evidence summary, three or four bullets with page references.>
Questions:
1. Do you recognise this from your side? Do you receive requests you cannot accommodate?
2. Were you informed about or involved in <the decision>?
3. Would you support <a letter / a statement> or share your experience with <the committee>?
<Sources with page numbers.>
```

## Follow-up after a fast reply

Purpose: keep momentum, give what was asked, ask one thing.

```
Thank you for the quick reply.
<Confirm the point that matters most in their reply, in their words.>
<What you will send and when: contacts, dossier, references.>
<One new fact from elsewhere in the campaign that helps them.>
<One question, or a proposed time for a call.>
```

## Update to an ally

Purpose: keep them informed, show progress, protect their name.

```
Short update.
<What was filed or sent, and what it covers. State that their name is not in it.>
<What came back from others, at the level the other party would accept being repeated.>
<What happens next and when you will report again.>
```

## Pitch to a news outlet

Purpose: story, document, human, deadline. One outlet per message.

```
Subject: Tip: <the contradiction in one line>

<Who writes, where they live or what they use, and what they did.>

The problem in five points:
1. <the rule and its stated reason, quoted>
2. <the evidence that it binds today>
3. <the institution's own advice against it>
4. <the announced but unpublished decision>
5. <the internal assessment, attributed to "parties in the area">

What has been requested: <formal requests with reference numbers, dates, and a compressed list of items>.
Decision dates: <dates>.

What I can offer: <dossier, PDFs, documents on arrival, contacts, availability>.

Sources (all public): <numbered list with page references and URLs>.

<Name, address, phone, e-mail>
```

## Formal information request, cover text

Purpose: precise enough to be answered, structured enough to be tracked.

```
Under <statute, article>, I request the documents specified below. They concern one matter: <one sentence>.
This request is separate from <earlier request, reference>; please register and decide on it separately.

Items (each with: what, why, where it is referenced, period):
01 ...
02 ...

Period: <per item>, through the date of your decision.
Form: digital, searchable, with an inventory list (number, date, author, subject, decision, exemption applied).
Exemptions: no objection to redacting staff below <level>, provided function and unit remain visible; otherwise motivate per document and passage.
Deadline: <statutory>; please confirm receipt with a case number.
Consultation: willing to prioritise (<items>) and to receive in phases.
```
